# Hyundai EV Copy Guidelines (Fictional)

> **Not an official Hyundai document.** This guide was written by students for the APAN 5560
> class project "Copywriting Assistant, EV Marketer". The voice rules, word lists and channel
> limits below are invented for the project. Hyundai does not endorse them.

Scope: ad copy for three EVs — **IONIQ 5**, **IONIQ 6** and **Kona Electric** — in the US market.

---

## 1. Brand Voice

We sound like a knowledgeable friend who owns an EV: calm, specific and on the reader's side.
Three traits guide every line.

### 1.1 Clear
- One idea per sentence. Short words over long ones.
- Explain a technical feature by what it does for the driver, then name it.
- Do: "Lift your foot and the car slows itself while sending energy back to the battery."
- Don't: "Advanced multi-level regenerative braking architecture optimizes energy recuperation."

### 1.2 Warm
- Talk to one person ("you"), not to "consumers" or "drivers everywhere".
- Show everyday moments: the school run, a weekend drive, a quiet morning commute.
- Do: "Charge at home overnight and leave every morning with a full day ahead."
- Don't: "Users can leverage home charging infrastructure for enhanced convenience."

### 1.3 Confident, not loud
- Let specific facts carry the excitement. No hype words, no stacked adjectives.
- At most one exclamation mark per piece of copy, and none in headlines.
- Do: "Fast charging that fits inside a coffee stop."
- Don't: "The most AMAZING, revolutionary charging experience EVER!!"

## 2. Audience

| Segment | What they want to hear | Typical model |
|---|---|---|
| First-time EV buyer | Charging is simple; daily range covers real life | Kona Electric |
| Growing family | Space, comfort, safety, easy road trips | IONIQ 5 |
| Design- and efficiency-minded commuter | Long range per charge, quiet cabin, distinctive look | IONIQ 6 |

Assume the reader is smart but not an engineer. Never make them feel behind for not knowing EV terms.

## 3. Vocabulary

### 3.1 Preferred terms

| Use | Instead of | Why |
|---|---|---|
| charge, charging | fuel up, refuel | EVs do not use fuel |
| range | mileage, tank | "mileage" means distance driven |
| estimated range | guaranteed range, real range | Range depends on conditions |
| zero tailpipe emissions | zero emissions, emission-free | Electricity generation can emit |
| Smart Cruise Control, Lane Following Assist, Highway Driving Assist | self-driving, autonomous, autopilot, hands-free | Names used in the manuals; see 3.2 |
| regenerative braking, Smart Regeneration System, i-PEDAL | one-pedal driving, brake-free driving | Hyundai's names; adjusted with the paddle shifters |
| V2L (vehicle-to-load) | power bank mode, generator mode | The manuals' term |
| Battery Conditioning | preconditioning, warm-up mode | The manuals' term |
| Bluelink, Digital Key | the Hyundai app, phone key | Names used in the manuals |
| Utility Mode | camp mode | The manuals' term |
| battery | power cell, energy pack | Plain word wins |
| DC charging, DC fast charging | supercharging, Supercharger | The manuals say "DC charging"; "Supercharger" is a competitor's brand |
| Level 2 charging, portable charging cable (ICCB) | home charger box, wall plug | Terms used in the manuals |

### 3.2 Model names
- Write **IONIQ 5** and **IONIQ 6** in capitals in copy, with a space before the number.
- Write **Kona Electric** (not "Kona EV" or "KONA E").
- Spec database names differ in case only: `Ioniq 5`, `Ioniq 6`, `Kona Electric`.

### 3.2.1 Feature rules
- Any line about Smart Cruise Control, Lane Following Assist or Highway Driving Assist must say the
  driver keeps hands on the wheel and stays in control. The manuals use "hands-free" only for the
  smart liftgate, never for driving.
- Any line about V2L must mention that using it reduces driving range.
- Features marked "(if equipped)" in the manual need "available" in copy.
- Towing differs by model: the IONIQ 6 and Kona Electric manuals do not recommend towing. Do not
  mention towing for those models.

### 3.3 Banned words and phrases
The off-brand checker flags these. Do not use them in any channel.

- revolutionary, game-changer, game-changing, disruptive
- best-in-class, #1, number one, unbeatable — unless a cited source is attached
- never worry, worry-free, unlimited, endless range
- self-driving, autonomous, hands-free driving, autopilot
- zero emissions, emission-free, 100% green, eco-friendly (vague)
- cheapest, lowest price, free charging (unless a dated offer is supplied)
- range anxiety (we describe the solution, not the fear)
- competitor brand or model names
- other brands' feature names: Autosteer, Traffic-Aware Cruise Control, Sentry Mode, Super Cruise, One-Pedal Driving, PowerBase, OnStar

## 4. Claims and Numbers

1. **Every number comes from the spec database.** Range, battery capacity, charging power,
   0–60 time, price and warranty years are looked up, never written from memory.
2. **How a feature works comes from the owner's manual.** Charging steps, regenerative braking,
   battery care and driver assistance descriptions must match the manual for that model.
3. **Never combine a database number with a manual number** in the same claim. If they disagree,
   use the database number and drop the manual number.
4. Range is always "estimated". Add "Actual range varies with conditions and driving habits."
   to any copy that states a range.
5. Charging time claims must name the condition (charger type and battery start/end level).
   If the condition is unknown, describe charging without a time.
6. Price claims say "starting at" and need the model year.
7. No comparisons with named competitors. Compare with the reader's current routine instead.

## 5. Model Messaging

Themes to lead with. Facts supporting each theme must still follow section 4.

### IONIQ 5
- Lead with: everyday versatility and a roomy, flexible cabin.
- Story: one car that handles the commute on weekdays and the road trip on weekends.
- Mood words: open, easygoing, ready.

### IONIQ 6
- Lead with: efficiency and a calm, streamlined design.
- Story: long, quiet drives where you stop to charge less often than you expect.
- Mood words: smooth, focused, unhurried.

### Kona Electric
- Lead with: an easy first step into electric driving.
- Story: city errands, simple home charging, a car that just fits your life.
- Mood words: friendly, nimble, simple.

## 6. Channel Rules

### 6.1 Social ad (Instagram, Facebook)
- Primary text: 125 characters or fewer.
- Headline: 40 characters or fewer.
- One call to action: "Explore IONIQ 5", "Find a dealer" or "Build yours".
- At most two hashtags and one emoji. No hashtags in the headline.

### 6.2 Search and display ad
- Headline: 30 characters or fewer. Description: 90 characters or fewer.
- Put the model name in the headline.
- At most one number per ad, taken from the spec database.

### 6.3 Email
- Subject line: 50 characters or fewer, no all caps, no exclamation mark.
- Preheader: 90 characters or fewer, adds new information to the subject.
- Body: 120–200 words in three parts — a relatable moment, two or three supporting facts,
  one call to action.
- Close with the range disclaimer from section 4 if a range is mentioned.

## 7. Examples

Numbers below are placeholders (`{range_miles}`, `{charging_speed_kw}`) filled from the spec database.

### 7.1 Social ad — IONIQ 5
**On brand**
> Headline: Weekdays to weekends, sorted
> Text: Room for the car seat, the groceries and the dog. Up to {range_miles} miles of estimated range. Explore IONIQ 5.

Why it works: one everyday story, one database number marked as estimated, one call to action.

**Off brand**
> Headline: The REVOLUTIONARY EV!!
> Text: Never worry about range again with unlimited self-driving fun!

Flags: all caps, two exclamation marks, "revolutionary", "never worry", "unlimited", "self-driving".

### 7.2 Search ad — IONIQ 6
**On brand**
> Headline: IONIQ 6: Built for Long Drives
> Description: Streamlined design and up to {range_miles} mi of estimated range. Find a dealer.

**Off brand**
> Headline: Best EV Sedan Ever
> Description: Beats every competitor with zero emissions and the cheapest price.

Flags: "best" without source, competitor comparison, "zero emissions", "cheapest".

### 7.3 Email — Kona Electric
**On brand**
> Subject: Your first EV can be this simple
> Preheader: Plug in at home, wake up charged, and get on with your day.
> Body opens with a morning routine, explains home charging in two sentences that match the
> owner's manual, states one database range figure with the disclaimer, and ends with "Build yours".
